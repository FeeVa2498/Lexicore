# Lexicore — Prototype

Playable browser prototype of the post-apocalyptic word-building roguelike from the Lexicore GDD.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a modern browser, or visit this repo's GitHub Pages URL.

## Story & world (roadmap)
- Humanity was wiped out in a war against alien invaders. Mutated animals, thinking plants and old defense robots roam the Earth.
- The Spell-Cat finds an ancient Tablet: English words become commands for a nanobot swarm.
Each of Acts 1–3 is a 9-floor map with 2 possible bosses and 2 elites (an elite never repeats within an act).

- **Act 1 — The Outskirts** (bosses: Ancient Stone Gargoyle or Mother Mycelia): overgrown nature at the edge of the ruins. Enemies are animals and plants that evolved after the war.
- **Act 2 — The Ruined City** (bosses: The Titan or the Gemini twins): enemies carry scavenged tools, weapons and armor.
- **Act 3 — The Tower** (bosses: Prisoner Zero or The Gatekeeper): enemies mix old-world and alien technology, including the **Prisoners** — captured aliens the Tower still holds for interrogation and research.
- **Act 4 — The Lexicore Chamber**: four fixed rooms (Rest Site → Shop → ? → LEXICORE).
- **Ending**: Lexicore reveals the climb was a test. The ancient English letters are nanobots, humanity's last weapon, and only a mind strong enough can command them. It copies the hero's mind into an army, launches the last missiles at the alien headquarters on the Moon, and ends with a closing line written for each hero.
- Each act has its own background map; shop and rest scenes use the current act's backdrop.
